# Anti-AI-slop rules

"AI slop" is UI whose decisions look generic, unjustified, repetitive or fabricated. This is a failure-pattern checklist, not a ban on individual techniques.

## Rounded-rectangle syndrome
Do not put every statistic, sentence, icon, setting and action in its own rounded card. Use hierarchy, sections, whitespace, dividers, rows, tables and bare layouts when better.

## Default AI aesthetic
Do not automatically add:
- purple/blue/cyan gradients;
- gradient headings;
- glassmorphism;
- glowing borders;
- blurred floating blobs;
- dotted/star backgrounds;
- huge shadows;
- random particles;
- shiny 3D objects;
- oversized radius.

Any can be valid if the local art direction calls for it. None means "premium" by default.

## Dashboard autopilot
Do not default to:
header → huge title → subtitle → stat cards → bento grid → CTA.

Choose composition from the real user task and information structure.

## Fake richness
Never fill space with invented activity feeds, avatars, counts, charts, testimonials, awards, integrations, social proof or "live" indicators.

## Decorative badges
Use badges for real compact status/category information. Do not invent NEW/PRO/SMART/AI/LIVE/SECURE labels.

## Icon bubble repetition
Do not put every icon in the same colored square/circle above a heading.

## Generic marketing copy
Do not invent phrases such as:
- Unlock your potential
- Everything you need in one place
- Powerful insights at your fingertips
- Take your experience to the next level
- Seamlessly manage your workflow
- Welcome back

unless intentionally approved product copy.

## Explaining obvious controls
Avoid text that merely restates a clear label and control.

Bad:
"Deposit funds — Use this section to deposit funds into your account..."

Better:
"Deposit"
[Amount]
[Deposit]

## Uniform templates
A settings page, game, analytics screen, checkout and landing page should not share one generic composition just for consistency. Share grammar; vary composition.

## Decoration stacking
Avoid gradient + glow + glass + noise + animated border + particles on one surface. Prefer one justified motif over many unrelated effects.

## Nested-card overload
Avoid cards inside cards inside cards unless nesting reflects real hierarchy.

## Excessive microcopy
Do not add helper text beneath every heading and field. Use it when it prevents confusion or aids recovery.

## Copying a kit's identity
When sourcing a component:
- preserve useful behavior;
- adapt tokens/proportions;
- remove demo copy;
- remove library-specific decoration;
- integrate it into local hierarchy.

The result must look like the project, not a library demo collage.

## Artificial originality
Do not create uniqueness through randomness. Prefer product-specific information architecture, typography, proportions, density, material, imagery, interaction and domain motifs.

## Final test
Ask:
- Does each visible element have a reason?
- Is any text just filling space?
- Is an effect here only because AI UIs often use it?
- Did the layout come from the task or from a familiar template?
- Would removing decoration improve clarity?
